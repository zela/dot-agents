# Evaluating mattpocock/skills

[mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) at v1.2.3 (2026-09-18): 25 promoted skills, 9 in-progress, 4 misc, MIT. Our `grill-me` is the early version of their `grill-me`; upstream has since split it into a reusable `grilling` primitive plus thin wrappers. Evaluated 2026-09-21 against the [skill flow](skill-flow.md).

## Verdict: same philosophy, different backbone. Take a third, harvest a third, skip a third.

The two kits agree on almost every principle: small composable skills, "understand the outcome not the instruction", design it twice, decisions written down, primary sources over summaries, terse prose (they even ban em-dashes repo-wide). Where they differ is structural, and the differences decide what can be taken verbatim:

| Their choice | Our choice | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Durable state lives on an **issue tracker** (GitHub, GitLab, or `.scratch/` markdown), configured once per repo by `setup-matt-pocock-skills`. | Durable state lives in **`docs/`** in the repo, host-agnostic, no setup step. | Their spec/ticket/triage/wayfinder chain is a parallel implementation of our Plan phase with a hard tracker dependency. Skip the chain, harvest its ideas. |
| Skills **commit** on completion (`implement`, `resolving-merge-conflicts`, `setup-pre-commit`, `prototype`). | Never stage or commit unless asked. | Every borrowed skill needs its commit step rewritten to "leave in the working tree". |
| Skills **delete** their own leftovers (`diagnosing-bugs` cleanup, `prototype` losing variants, `wizard` when done). | Never delete without explicit consent, including our own artifacts. | Same: rewrite to "list what should go, wait for a yes". |
| Some outputs are deliberately **ephemeral** (`improve-codebase-architecture` HTML to `$TMPDIR`, `handoff` to the OS temp dir). | Every phase writes to `docs/` so a `/clear` loses nothing. | Fine for a handoff file that travels. Wrong for a survey, which belongs in the `census` ledger. |
| **User-invoked vs model-invoked** is an explicit design axis: user-only skills set `disable-model-invocation: true` and pay zero context load. | Every custom skill carries a full trigger description, always loaded. | A structural idea worth applying to our own kit regardless of what else is taken. |

## Per-skill verdicts

**Adopt as-is** means one entry in `upstream-sources.txt`, kept current by `bootstrap.sh --upstream`. **Vendor with edits** means copy into `custom-skills/` and adjust to our commit/delete rules and house style. **Harvest** means take one idea into an existing skill or doc, no new skill.

### Productivity

| Skill | What it does | Overlap here | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `grilling` | The interview primitive. Models the plan as a design tree, asks the whole **frontier** (every question whose prerequisites are settled) per round, numbers each question with a recommended answer, dispatches subagents for facts, reserves decisions for the user, and is done only when the frontier is empty. | Our `grill-me` is the ancestor: same intent, "one question at a time", "explore the codebase instead of asking". | **Upgrade `grill-me` in place.** Take the frontier model, the fact-versus-decision split with subagent dispatch, the explicit done criterion, and "do not act until the user confirms". One decision for the user: keep one-at-a-time, or switch to rounds. Rounds are principled (a dependent question is excluded by construction) but upstream issue #44 ("Codex just asked me 200 questions") shows they can balloon. Recommendation: rounds, since the frontier definition is the cap; keep the `❓`/`➡️` format optional. Stay single-file, no wrapper split: nothing else here invokes it yet. |
| `grill-me` | One line: invoke `grilling`. | | **Skip.** Ours becomes the primitive. |
| `to-questionnaire` | Turn a decision that someone else holds the facts for into a markdown questionnaire. Grills the user about the **send** (who, what you need back), not the subject, then writes most-important-first questions with answer stubs. | None. ## Ideas worth taking that are not skills

- **Invocation as a design axis.** A skill only a human types should not spend context on a trigger description. Their `SKILL-MECHANICS.md` states the trade; our kit never makes it.
- **Primitive plus wrappers.** `grilling` is reached by `grill-me`, `grill-with-docs`, `triage`, `wayfinder`, `improve-codebase-architecture` via "call the Skill tool with X". One source of truth for the interview. Our equivalent is `verifier` reached by `review-staged` and `census`.
- **Soft versus hard dependency** (their ADR-0001). A skill that only sharpens output when a doc exists says so in vague prose and works without it. A skill that cannot function without config names the setup step once. Everything recommended above is soft.
- **Phase boundaries as an ordered tree.** Continue if the next phase needs this one as a primary source. `/clear` if nothing here matters. `/handoff` only when something travels. Subagent if the task runs AFK. `/compact` otherwise, with an instruction. The order is the value.
- **`.out-of-scope/` as institutional memory.** They record rejected feature requests for their own repo, with the reason and every issue that asked. `docs/notes/*-eval.md` plays that role here for skills considered and not taken.
- **A repo-wide no-em-dash rule**, enforced in `CLAUDE.md`. Same house style as ours; theirs is written down.

## Outcome (2026-09-21)

Everything above was applied, with one exception: the repo-wide no-em-dash rule was not taken. Em-dashes stay in prose. `AGENTS.md` now states the split: technical text that a reader follows exactly (docs, runbooks, `SKILL.md`, agent instructions) gets the `simple-english` discipline, and prose stays normal English.

1. `upstream-sources.txt` gained `mattpocock/skills tdd research to-questionnaire handoff writing-for-agents teach`, and the six installed. The CLI resolved `--skill <name>` against the nested layout, so the assumption in the first draft of this note held. `wizard` stays deferred.
2. `custom-skills/` gained `diagnosing-bugs`, `prototype`, `resolving-merge-conflicts`, and `domain-modeling` (glossary half only), each rewritten in plain English with the commit and delete steps changed to propose-and-wait, and a footer crediting upstream.
3. `grill-me` moved to the frontier model: a design tree worked in rounds, facts fetched by the agent, decisions left to the user, done when the frontier is empty. Rounds, not one-at-a-time, with a soft cap of five or six questions per round.
4. Harvests landed: dependency categories, the three tests, and subagent-per-variant in `design-an-interface`; a module-depth lens in `census`'s category 5; expand–contract and the fog-or-session test in `multi-session-plan`, with a pointer from `run-state`; the Spec axis in `review-mr` and `review-staged`; the Merge Danger description template in `review-mr`; the phase-boundary tree and the `CONTEXT.md` line in `AGENTS.md`.
5. `census`, `review-mr`, `review-staged`, `run-state`, and `ship-session` carry `disable-model-invocation: true` plus an `agents/openai.yaml` policy for Codex. `grill-me` and `multi-session-plan` keep model invocation, because their descriptions carry natural-language triggers ("grill me", "plan this out") that the user relies on. The git guardrail lives at `hooks/block-dangerous-git.sh`, wired first under the `Bash` matcher in `~/.claude/settings.json`, with plain `git push` allowed and force pushes blocked.
6. [skill-flow.md](skill-flow.md) shows the new skills in their phases. Its HTML renders were not regenerated.
